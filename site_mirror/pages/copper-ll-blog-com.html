A stylish kitchen with the NEW YORK COPPER II

A stylish kitchen with the New York Copper II

Copper is a warm metallic hue that brings an air of sophistication and style to any interior. Whether you're designing a sleek, dark kitchen or a light, minimalist space with marble accents, copper elements can transform any kitchen into a tasteful focal point. That's why we're excited to offer our New York sink and matching accessories in the stunning Copper II finish! 

Bedrijfsbrochure

A striking centrepiece for any kitchen

The sink is no longer just a functional necessity; it's now an integral part of kitchen design. Whether your style is modern, industrial or classic, the New York sink in Copper II adds a touch of luxury and character. This copper sink will be the standout feature in any kitchen.

The quality you expect from Reginox

Since 1976, we have been crafting high-quality stainless steel sinks, worktops and kitchen accessories. Naturally, the same applies to the New York sink in Copper II. The distinctive copper colour is achieved using PVD technology – where an ultra-thin layer is applied to the stainless steel.

This PVD coating is not only aesthetically superior but also durable and highly resistant to wear. The colour remains beautiful for a long time, retains its deep shine and is more resistant to wear than traditional paint or powder coatings. However, we do recommend avoiding aggressive substances such as toothpaste or chlorine because prolonged exposure may cause discolouration.

Unique Detail: At Reginox, we handle the entire PVD process in-house. Thanks to our in-house PVD machine, we can coat our sinks in luxury finishes such as Copper, Gold and Gun Metal, , resulting in a beautiful, elegant shine. A fine example of craftsmanship.

A stainless steel sink with colour: the best of both worlds

The New York Copper II sink is made from stainless steel, topped with a warm copper-coloured finish. This means you benefit from all the advantages of stainless steel:

  • Non-porous, that means bacteria and stains cannot penetrate it.
  • Easy to clean.
  • Scratch & wear resistant
  • Long-lasting, even with daily, intensive use.

With the New York sink in Copper II, you get a truly eye-catching feature that's also functional, hygienic and durable. The best of both worlds!

Combine the sink with other Copper II-coloured elements

Complete the look with our matching Copper II accessories. These too are coated with a PVD finish, creating a harmonious, cohesive design. Pair the copper sink and accessories with a beautiful floor and worktop for a luxurious, cohesive feel in your kitchen.
