REGINOX CELEBRATES
50 YEARS OF CRAFTMANSHIP

Reginox was founded in April 1976 by Mr. Arend Jan ter Steege in a small shed in Rijssen. What started as a modest family business with a clear ambition – to produce high-quality stainless steel sinks – has grown into an international brand synonymous with quality, design and sustainability. From the very beginning, craftsmanship has been key. With a passion for perfection and an eye for detail, Reginox has managed to distinguish itself in a competitive market.

FOLLOWING 
TRENDS &
DEVELOPMENTS 

Over the years, Reginox has continuously adapted to changing trends and technological developments. Innovation has always been the driving force behind our success. From the first classic sinks to modern, stylish solutions that combine functionality and design, Reginox continues to respond to the wishes of consumers worldwide. Today, our products can be found in kitchens in more than 80 countries, proof of our international character and our commitment to quality.

HALF A CENTURY
OF PROGRESS

The 50th anniversary is not only a time to look back, but above all to look ahead. Reginox continues to invest in new technologies, sustainable materials and smart designs. Our mission is clear: to create kitchen solutions that make everyday life more beautiful and easier. Together with our partners and customers, we are building a future where style, functionality and sustainability go hand in hand.

PROUD OF 
OUR ROOTS

Although Reginox operates worldwide, we remain proud of our origins in Twente. The family feeling and commitment that have been present in the company from the very beginning are still at the heart of our identity. This anniversary is a tribute to our history and a promise for the future: Reginox will continue to innovate, inspire and deliver quality – today, tomorrow and for the next 50 years.
