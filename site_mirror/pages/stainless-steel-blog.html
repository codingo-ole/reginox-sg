Why STAINLESS STEEL-SINKS are indispensable in a kitchen

Stainless steel sinks are indispensable

Choosing a kitchen is not something you do every day. That is why it is very important to inspire and support your customers in making the right choices for their kitchen design. This page supports your efforts with full information about our stainless steel sinks, worktops, taps and associated accessories. Everything you want to know about the quality of our products and the endless possibilities to combine our products, all in one clear overview. A true source of inspiration!

Bedrijfsbrochure

WHY CHOOSE STAINLESS STEEL IN THE KITCHEN?

Stainless steel has been one of the most popular materials in the kitchen for many years. This is partly due to its timeless and stylish appearance. In addition, stainless steel is easy to clean, durable and does not get damaged easily. The perfect choice for an intensively used kitchen!

STAINLESS STEEL SINKS AND ACCESSORIES OF TOP QUALITY 

We have been producing top quality stainless steel sinks, worktops and accessories since 1976. The sink is one of the key components of the kitchen. After all, that is the place where we wash our hands, food and dishes. In addition, a sink stands out in the kitchen. That is why a sink should be functional, easy to clean and maintain, and stylish. 

WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF STAINLESS STEEL SINKS?

Our stainless steel sinks offer the ideal combination of the above properties.  Stainless steel is non-porous. That means bacteria and stains cannot penetrate it. It is easy to clean with a damp cloth and a few drops of liquid soap. The material is so robust that the sink is not easily damaged. It will last for many years. Even with intensive use. And thanks to its timeless and stylish look and feel, stainless steel fits into any kitchen.

DEEPDRAW PROCESS STAINLESS STEEL SINKS

Our stainless steel sinks are also deep-drawn. This specific deep-drawing process allows the water to drain much better and no water remains in the sink. A unique and major advantage!

ENDLESS COMBINATIONS

Reginox designs, develops and produces almost all its products in-house in a range of styles and designs. We have a wide variety of coloured sinks that match other products in our range. This includes a colander, pop-up set, soap dispenser, strainer and a bottom grid. This allows for infinite combinations of our products. You can find a suitable combination for any kitchen requirement. Our mood board offers a good portion of inspiration! Curious about our full range? Check it out here!
